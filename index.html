TERMER ONE · THE TRANSITION

The Crucible & De-Civilianization

The journey commenced at the historic gates of the Nigerian Defence Academy in Afaka, Kaduna. Stepping into Term One demanded the total relinquishing of civilian comforts in exchange for relentless military regimentation. Pre-dawn bugle calls at 0430 hours began each day with punishing Physical Training (PT), speed marches across Ribadu campus, and endurance drills under stern Academy instructors.

On the sacred drill square, recruits learned precision foot drills in uniform, absolute obedience, rifle handling, and the foundational Academy ethos: Loyalty and Valour.

Key Focus: Drill Square Discipline, Physical Conditioning, Academy Ethos

TERMER TWO · FOUNDATIONS & BATTALION

Academic Rigor & Dalet Battalion Spirit

Term Two brought the rigorous fusion of demanding degree-level academic coursework and tactical military doctrine. Days were divided between engineering, defense studies, and grueling field exercises in the Kaduna bush.

Inducted proudly into Dalet Battalion, Cadet A.A. Lawal embraced the battalion's legendary motto: Adaptability and Teamwork. Mastering map reading, tactical camouflage, navigation, and endurance patrols, the cadet identity took permanent root.

TERMER THREE · THE FORGE

Tactical Mastery & Comrades in Arms

As the middle stretch of training peaked, the physical and mental demands escalated drastically. Battle Physical Efficiency Tests (BPET), assault obstacle courses, tactical river crossings, and live section attack drills tested character to its core.

Cadet Lawal exemplified deep composure and mental tenacity, seated firmly in the Dalet heritage. In this demanding forge, the profound brotherhood of military service was cemented among comrades who shared every drop of sweat, water canteen, and tactical trial.

TERMER FOUR · LEADERSHIP & COMMAND

Senior Cadet Authority & Combat Readiness

Stepping into senior cadet appointments with yellow/gold chevron brassards, Term Four transitioned Cadet Lawal from executing directives to conceiving, commanding, and coordinating tactical missions. Senior cadets oversaw junior cohorts and led advanced field maneuvers.

Wearing combat ballistic helmets with camouflage netting, Senior Cadet A.A. Lawal and his senior peers stood prepared for combat readiness, live-fire battle orders, and rapid tactical deployments under intense operational pressure.

TERMER FIVE · CAMP HIGHLAND & COMMISSION

Camp Highland & The Commissioning Parade

The ultimate baptism of military readiness: Exercise Camp Highland. Stretching across the unforgiving highlands and rugged bush of Kachia, Jos, and Kaduna, cadets undertook grueling mountain climbs, multi-day tactical survival drills, and long-range tactical marches in full fighting kit.

Having conquered every test with unshakeable resolve, Cadet Adam Ayomide Lawal marched proudly onto the Afaka parade ground before the Commander-in-Chief and cheering families. Stepping onto the parade ground as a cadet and marching off as a commissioned Second Lieutenant in the Armed Forces of Nigeria.

Getting There
The Passing Out Parade takes place within the NDA Afaka Campus, Kaduna. Please note the entry protocols and transport details below.
Strict Security Notice: Entry to the Academy grounds is strictly by official invitation card. Every adult guest must present a physical invitation pass along with a valid government-issued ID at the gate. Please arrive early to clear security inspection.
STEP 1

From Mando / Kawo Park

Board a registered vehicle or taxi heading towards Airport Road. Both motor parks operate frequent shuttles on this route — inform the driver you are stopping at "NDA Main Gate, Afaka."

STEP 2

Along Airport Road

Stay along Airport Road past the National Eye Centre corridor until you arrive at the NDA Afaka Main Gate, which features prominent military sentry towers and signages.

STEP 3

Security Clearance Gate

Proceed to the visitor clearance pavilion. Present your physical invitation card and identification to military police personnel for credential verification and tagging.

STEP 4

Internal Parade Shuttle

Once cleared through security, board the official internal Academy shuttle buses that run continuously to the main parade grounds and guest grandstands.
